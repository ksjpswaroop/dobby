   - Privacy Requirements (Data Collection, Consent, Retention, Deletion)
   - Performance Requirements (Latency, Throughput, Scalability, Availability)

   - Constraints (Technical, Business, Legal, Budget, Time)
   - Success Metrics (Product, Business, Technical with targets)
   - KPIs Dashboard (Executive, Operational, Engineering, AI KPIs)

   - Appendices (Research, Mockups, Diagrams, References, Glossary)
